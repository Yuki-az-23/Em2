Key behaviours proven by integration tests:

- **Quotas:** with 3 messages an hour, the fourth publish is a `429`, and an ingress request to an
  unknown handle is a `429` too; with 1 egress fetch a day, the second is a `429`. The ledger shows
  3 of 3 used and no reputation.
- **Probing:** six near-threshold variants from one sender to one receiver: the first four pass, the
  fifth and sixth are denied (reason names the probe) and never reach the receiver's feed or
  deferred list, every sender response is identical, the observer sees 4 passes, 2 denies, 1 probe
  and a 0.25 penalty, and the prober's hourly quota drops from 120 to 30. A non-observer gets 404 on
  another principal's ledger.
- **Reconciliation:** after a pass and a deny are counted, an injected `passes = 99` is repaired by
  the job, the run is recorded, and `em2_ledger_drift_total` is non-zero.
- **Snapshots:** the stored snapshot validates against the published observer schema.
- **Card:** shows handle, kind and emotion only; hiding removes the emotion; unknown ids get 404.
- **Shape limit:** of a risky flood, one shaped copy is delivered and the rest wait with the hold
  reason; when the interval has passed, the job delivers the next one.
- **bridging_vector:** content delivered to two far receivers twice reaches bridging 1 on the third
  copy's dossier.

## Differences from the blueprint

- Reputation is not shown to the principal it is about (ADR-014): the blueprint's
  `GET /v1/me/ledger` included it, which would let a sender read receivers' verdicts.
- Probe detection lives in the verdict engine, per sender and receiver, not in the content dossier.
- `liberty_pressure` (receivers' bridges moving negative after exposure) needs exposure history and
  moves to M6 with the journal and wellbeing.
- Quotas have no per-principal override endpoint; base values come from configuration
  (`EM2_QUOTA_MESSAGES_PER_HOUR`, `EM2_QUOTA_EGRESS_PER_DAY`).
- The ledger snapshot has no deferred count or pressure contributed: those are verdict data, and the
  ledger may not depend on verdict (verdict depends on the ledger).
